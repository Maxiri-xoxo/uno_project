A TALE OF 2 CITIES

Mira had always believed her city—Lunaris—was a quiet place, a place where shadows behaved and secrets stayed buried. Jonas, living across the river in the bustling city of Solmere, believed the opposite: that truth was loud, restless, and impossible to hide. They met by accident—if accidents existed—on the old bridge connecting the two worlds. Mira had gone there searching for silence; Jonas had been chasing a rumor. The rumor was about a storm. Not one of wind or rain, but of people vanishing without a trace. At first, Mira wanted nothing to do with Jonas’s theories. He talked fast, thought faster, and carried danger like a spark in his pocket. But when someone familiar to her disappeared, she found herself walking back to the bridge, hoping he’d be there. He was. Jonas always seemed to be exactly where trouble began. Together, they followed a trail of strange clues: lights flickering at midnight, abandoned buildings humming with energy, and messages carved into stone that both cities had tried to erase. The deeper they went, the more Mira realized she had lived her whole life overlooking the truth—and that Jonas had spent his running toward it. Their search finally led them to the old observatory perched between the two cities. Inside, they uncovered the truth: a hidden group manipulating energy between Lunaris and Solmere, draining life from both sides to keep themselves powerful. Exposing the group meant danger, but retreating meant the cities would fall. So they chose to fight. Side by side, they brought the truth into the light. The storm broke—not with destruction, but with awakening. And when the cities finally began to heal, Mira and Jonas found themselves back on the bridge where it all began. This time, they didn’t arrive alone. They arrived together.
